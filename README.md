# MATHVERSE 2.0 — The Universal Mathematical Universe & Calculus Academy

MATHVERSE 2.0 is a comprehensive, offline-first, interactive mathematics learning, simulation, and Olympiad preparation platform.

---

## 🌟 Core Architecture & Feature Matrix

### Part 1: All Core Features & Systems
- **3D Mathematical Universe**: Interactive Three.js celestial space with planetary nodes representing mathematical realms, orbital animations, and constellation lines.
- **Interactive Mathematical Topologies**: 3D interactive models of Clifford Torus, Möbius Strip, Lorenz Strange Attractor, and Calabi-Yau 3-Fold projections.
- **Personal Mathematics Tutor (Hypatia)**: AI-driven conversational guidance with both direct analytical explanations and Socratic Discovery Mode.
- **Socratic Thinking Mode**: Guides students with deep questions rather than giving away solutions.
- **Olympiad Training Center**: Competition problem archive with 4-Tier Progressive Hints and complete analytical proofs.
- **Mathematical Boss Battles**: Turn-based RPG boss fights (The Limit Guardian, Derivative Master, Chain Rule Architect, Optimization Engineer, Integral Alchemist, Substitution Specialist, Infinite Challenger).
- **Mathematical Duels**: Real-time 1v1 duels against AI grandmasters (Euler, Gauss, Ramanujan, Noether) with an active ELO rating system.
- **Infinite Problem Generator**: Procedural generator producing randomized problems with exact verified solutions.
- **Mathematical Detective**: "Find the Flaw" crime scene cases exposing illegal steps in bogus proofs (e.g. 1 = 2, -1 = 1).
- **Proof Arena & Studio**: Drag-and-drop / deductive step builder supporting Proof by Contradiction, Delta-Epsilon limit proofs, and Mathematical Induction.
- **Personal Weakness Radar**: 8-axis interactive spider radar chart mapping proficiency across Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, Combinatorics, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Proof Rigor, and Speed.
- **Student Dashboard & Analytics**: Persistent XP, Level progression, Streak tracking, and achievement trophies.

---

### Part 2: Dedicated Calculus Academy
- **Division A: Differential Calculus**
  - **Limits**: Epsilon-Delta definitions, one-sided limits, algebraic factoring & conjugates, trigonometric limits, Squeeze Theorem, limits at infinity, L'Hôpital's Rule.
  - **Continuity**: Point-wise continuity (3 conditions), interval continuity, removable/jump/infinite discontinuities, Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT).
  - **Differentiation Fundamentals**: First principles limit definition, Power rule, Constant rule, Sum/Difference rules, Product rule, Quotient rule, Chain rule, Implicit differentiation, Logarithmic differentiation.
  - **Derivatives of Function Families**: Polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, and inverse trigonometric functions.
  - **Applications of Derivatives**: Tangent and normal lines, instantaneous rates of change, related rates, critical points, concavity, inflection points, optimization, Rolle's Theorem, and Mean Value Theorem (MVT).
- **Division B: Integral Calculus**
  - **Integration Fundamentals**: Antiderivatives, indefinite integrals, constant of integration (+ C), basic power rule, exponential and trigonometric integrals.
  - **Integration Techniques**: U-Substitution, Integration by Parts (LIATE rule), Partial Fraction Decomposition, and Improper Integrals.
  - **Definite Integrals**: Riemann sums (Left, Right, Midpoint, Trapezoidal), net signed area, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC Part 1 & FTC Part 2).
  - **Applications of Integration**: Area between curves, average value of a function, and volumes of solids of revolution (Disk and Washer methods).

### Part 4: Interactive Calculus Laboratory
- **Derivative Visualizer**: Dynamic function graphing, interactive tangent line gliding along curve at $x_0$, slope $m = f'(x_0)$ readout, tangent equation, and concurrent $f'(x)$ derivative curve.
- **Riemann Integral Visualizer**: Subinterval sliders ($n = 2$ to $80$), comparison of Left, Right, Midpoint, and Trapezoidal sums, positive/negative signed area color coding, and error convergence $\Delta \to 0$ against exact benchmark.
- **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC) Explorer**: Manipulate the upper bound $x$ to observe the accumulated area function $F(x) = \int_a^x f(t) dt$ and simultaneously verify that the rate of change $F'(x)$ equals the curve height $f(x)$!
